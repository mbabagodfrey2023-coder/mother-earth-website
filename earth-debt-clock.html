Important note on estimates. The Earth Debt Clock presents scientific estimates, not audited figures. The total debt figure carries a wide uncertainty range ($100T–$500T) reflecting different assumptions about restoration technology costs, ecosystem valuation methods, and discount rates. Ticking counters for CO₂, trees, plastic, and topsoil are rate-based interpolations from annual published figures — the same methodology used by the US National Debt Clock. Live CO₂ data is sourced directly from NOAA Mauna Loa via MEKE AI's GAIA data feed. All sources are peer-reviewed or from authoritative international bodies. This clock is an awareness tool; for scientific analysis, consult the primary sources linked above. Data sourced and maintained by MEKE AI Environmental Intelligence — Nairobi, Kenya.
